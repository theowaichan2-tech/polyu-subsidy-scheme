Frequently asked

Questions before applying.

Who can apply? +−

Current full-time PolyU School of Design students may apply. Exchange students are not eligible.

What support can I request? +−

You can request an entrance fee subsidy and a shipping allowance for sending physical work.

Is there a funding cap? +−

Yes. Each student has a HK$10,000 cycle cap. Shipping support is capped at HK$1,000 per application.

Do I need to report results? +−

Yes. Report each official competition result within one month of its announcement.
